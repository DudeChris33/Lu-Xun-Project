# Citations

## Primary text

**Lu Xun.** "Diary of a Madman." 1918. English translation by Yang Hsien-yi and Gladys Yang. The PDF at the project root (`Diary of a Madman.pdf`) is the working text for in-game quotes.

## Secondary sources — 2018 framing

**Human Rights Watch.** *China's Algorithms of Repression: Reverse Engineering a Xinjiang Police Mass Surveillance App.* May 1, 2019. https://www.hrw.org/report/2019/05/01/chinas-algorithms-repression/reverse-engineering-xinjiang-police-mass

Anchor source for the surveillance-state argument used in the 2018 mode and About screen.

## Conventions

In-game placeholder quote slots (e.g., `[QUOTE: 1918 game-over — TBD]` in `game/themes.py`) will be replaced with quoted text plus a short source key, e.g. `[Lu Xun 1918 / Yang trans.]`.

When adding a new quote or paraphrase to any string in `game/themes.py` or `game/content.py`, add the full source here first and reference it by short key in a comment near the quote.
